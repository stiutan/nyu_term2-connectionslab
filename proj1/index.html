A virtual library of poisonous books

Some books
should be judged
by their covers.

This is a virtual collection of mass-produced, nineteenth century (1800s) books tested to have arsenical emerald green pigments in their bookbindings. These toxic, heavy metal, components may be in their book cloth, paper wrapping or covering, endpapers, paper labels or onlays, leather onlays, and textblock edges.
